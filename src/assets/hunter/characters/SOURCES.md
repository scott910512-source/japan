Original characters and artwork belong to their respective rights holders.

Source: [HUNTER×HUNTER NEN×IMPACT official character pages](https://hunterhunter-ni.bushiroad.com/character/gon/).

The same official asset directory supplies all five characters:
`https://hunterhunter-ni.bushiroad.com/wordpress/wp-content/themes/hunter-ni_v1/assets/images/common/character/`

For each of `gon`, `killua`, `kurapika`, `leorio`, `hisoka`:

- `{name}.png`: `img_{name}_tachie.png`
- `{name}-thumb.png`: `thumb_{name}.png`

No generative replacements or redraws are used. Theme assets can be replaced independently of learning records.

## Early-story lesson teachers

Official 2011 anime character portraits from Nippon Television:

- [Hunter Exam](https://www.ntv.co.jp/hunterhunter/character/other_01.html):
  `satotsu.gif`, `hanzo.gif`, `menchi.gif`, `buhara.gif`, `netero.gif`.
- [Heavens Arena](https://www.ntv.co.jp/hunterhunter/character/other_03.html):
  `wing.gif`, `zushi.gif`.
- [Zoldyck family](https://www.ntv.co.jp/hunterhunter/character/other_02.html):
  `canary.gif` (original filename `other_kanaria.gif`).

Original image URL: `https://www.ntv.co.jp/hunterhunter/character/images/other_{name}.gif`.
These are original, unmodified GIF files. The UI shows the circular portrait at the
top using a clipped CSS viewport; no generated faces or synthetic expressions are used.
Learning roles, encouraging lines and student questions are original app copy, not
quotes or official teaching roles. Source dialogue and kanji facts remain in the
existing learning data. All eight GIFs are included in the offline cache.
